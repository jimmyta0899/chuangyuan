A mechanical and electrical equipment supplier built on sourcing discipline

Chuang Yuan procures and trades high - end electromechanical equipment, valves, meters, communication terminal equipment and electronic automation products for projects in China and abroad.

Who we are

Guangzhou Chuangyuan Mechanical and Electrical Equipment Co., Ltd. was established in 2015. We work as a high-quality electromechanical equipment supplier, handling domestic procurement together with import and export trade for buyers who need equipment sourced to a defined specification.

Our work covers high - end electromechanical equipment, valves, meters, communication terminal equipment and electronic automation products. We have taken part in many large-scale projects at home and abroad, and we are a qualified supplier for well-known central enterprises such as China Communications Construction Company (CCCC), China Energy Engineering Group Co., Ltd. and China Railway International Co., Ltd.

What we hold ourselves to

Our stated philosophy is that quality changes everything and service achieves the future. In practice that means matching each order to a named brand and a defined model, confirming the documentation a project requires, and keeping communication with the buyer's technical team direct.
